# 0197 — The reaction is the terminal-message action

Date: 2026-09-02, with unit 54.

## Context

Decision 0155 (2026-08-30) placed the reaction's home on unaddressed chatter that lands — a
share, a milestone, a joke — and rejected the earlier design's terminal-message fit, the
thank-you and the "that worked". With unit 54 the operator redefined the reaction while
correcting the unit's teaching, and the definition is the one 0155 rejected, restored on the
operator's own words:

> Call it a terminal-message action. If a response to the assistant needs no further response
> it can be stamped off with a reaction.
> Something like
> A: Xenia how does x and y work
> Xenia: this and that way
> A: Thanks
> Xenia reacts with ✌️

Asked whether this replaces the chatter wording: "My thing replaces that yes".

## Decision

The reaction is the terminal-message action: a response to the assistant that needs no
further response can be stamped off with a reaction. The sentence is permissive — can, never
should — and the standing bounds survive untouched: words and a reaction never land on one
message, one message takes at most one reaction ever, most messages deserve none, and
silence stays the default. Decision 0155's fit clause is superseded by this one; its bounds
live on here.

## Rejected

- **The chatter fit of 0155.** Superseded on the operator's decision above; the chatter that
  lands now draws nothing, which the silence default already covers.
- **Keeping both fits.** One teaching cannot name two different triggers for one act.
