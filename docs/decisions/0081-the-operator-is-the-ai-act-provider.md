# The operator is the AI Act provider, and the position is a tracked record

Date: 2026-08-23. Unit 12, the first-interaction disclosure.

The operator is the provider of the assistant under the AI Act, not merely
a deployer: the assistant is assembled from a general-purpose model, given
its own purpose and put into service under the operator's name — the third
example of §2.3 of the Commission's Article 50 guidelines. The scope exits
fail: Article 2(10) (purely personal, non-professional use) does not cover
a public community service, and Article 2(12) (free and open-source
release) covers releasing, not operating.

The position lives as a tracked compliance record beside the privacy
documents: the role conclusion with its grounds, the obligations map
(minimal risk — no Annex III category, Article 5 clear — with Article 50's
duties applying), the Article 50(2) marking position (upstream text
marking relied on per the guidelines' permitted path, the due-diligence
check recorded as OPEN pending the first live turn, the under-200-token
practice bound of the marking Code of Practice noted), the detection route
through the public industry-standard detector, the gap analysis in place
of Code of Practice accession (para 148), and the Article 4 literacy note
— the operator built and operates the system, and this repository's
documents are the ongoing literacy record. The impact assessment's role
wording corrects from deployer to provider with a dated note.

## Rejected alternatives

- **The deployer reading.** Comfortable and wrong: a deployer uses
  someone else's system; this assistant is its own system, put into
  service by the operator. The guidelines' own example decides it.
- **Acceding to the Code of Practice.** Oversized for a one-operator
  community assistant; para 148 permits demonstrating compliance by other
  adequate means, and the recorded gap analysis is that means.
- **A policy-page disclosure instead of the in-system line.** The
  guidelines rule the placement out by name; the compliance record only
  documents what the mechanism (decisions 0078 and 0079) already does.
